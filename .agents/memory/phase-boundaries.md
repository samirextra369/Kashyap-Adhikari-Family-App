---
name: Phase boundaries
description: Delivery sequencing for the Kashyap Adhikari genealogy platform
---

The product is intentionally delivered family-first: establish the mobile genealogy experience and data-governance shape before adding community feed, events, chat, or other social modules.

**Why:** The requirements explicitly prioritize accurate, traceable genealogy data, privacy, and administration above social features.

**How to apply:** Keep later phases isolated behind clear domain boundaries; do not let demo or social data become the source of truth for verified genealogy.
